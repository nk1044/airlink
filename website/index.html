Battery at a glance

Noise control in one click

Noise Cancellation, Transparency or Off. The selection slides over and settles only when your buds confirm. Long‑press a bud and Earshot follows along.

There when they are

Earshot hides from the menu bar until your buds connect for audio, and disappears again when they leave. No Dock icon, no windows, no scanning.

Feels like it came with your Mac.

Built with SwiftUI and AppKit to match the system's own menu bar panels, in Light and Dark. Every control works with VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion swaps slides for cross‑fades.

Honest by design.

If your buds won't switch modes, say because only one is in your ear, Earshot tells you why in one line instead of pretending. Levels the buds don't report show as "—", never a guess.

Featherweight.

No polling, no timers, no background scanning. Your buds tell Earshot when something changes.

Install in a minute.

  1. Download

    Earshot.dmg, open it, and drag Earshot to Applications.

  2. Open it once

    Earshot is free and isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks first. Open it, click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. Allow Bluetooth

    Earshot needs Bluetooth to talk to your buds. Connect them, and the icon appears in your menu bar.

Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Earshot.app does the same as step 2.

Questions.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apple only skips the warning for apps signed with a paid Developer ID and notarized. Earshot is a free, open‑source project, so macOS asks you to confirm once. The full source and the build are on GitHub, and every release is built there by GitHub Actions.

Does it work with other earbuds?

Out of the box it supports realme Buds Air 6 Pro. The app is built around per‑model profiles, so other buds that use the same realme/OPPO protocol can be added with one file.

Will it drain my Mac's battery?

No. While your buds are away, Earshot only waits for a Bluetooth connect notification. While they're connected, it sits at 0.0 % CPU and reacts only to messages from the buds.

Why does noise cancellation sometimes refuse?

The buds themselves only allow noise cancellation with both buds in your ears, and the other modes with at least one. Earshot tells you which one it is.

Can I use it alongside realme Link on my phone?

Yes. Earshot only talks to your buds through the Mac's own connection.
